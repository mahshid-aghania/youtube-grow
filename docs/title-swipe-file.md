## The formula (for writing more)

First-person or underdog subject + **em-dash reveal** (`[humble setup] — [shocking payoff]`)
+ single emoji pattern-interrupt. Keep the payoff implied, not spoiled. Pair with a
bright, single-face, shocked-expression thumbnail and short overlay text.
